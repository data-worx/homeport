How it works

  1. 1

    Pull

    Get the compose file. Images are signed and come from one registry.

  2. 2

    docker compose up

    Gateway, registry and admin portal start. Connect your IdP.

  3. 3

    Dock modules

    From the catalog or your own services. Each registers itself and is routed, metered and observed.

# Quickstart
$ curl -fsSLO https://homeport.data-worx.de/compose.yaml
$ docker compose up -d
